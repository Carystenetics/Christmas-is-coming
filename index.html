Christmas is coming, my love

For you, who made every month before it feel like Christmas.

–days
–hours
–minutes
–seconds

November, You

the month she carried us

November came in grey and early dark,

and there you were, already up,

the coffee made, the lamp turned on,

my coat warm on the chair, my cup

filled before I thought to ask.

You work so hard, you never say

how heavy all the hours get,

you simply carry them away.

The first cold wind could try its worst.

Christmas is coming, and I know

it started here, in this plain month,

with you, the one who loves me so.

1

Hands That Never Rest

on the nights you come home tired

I watch you set your bag down slow,

the day still written in your eyes.

You give the world your whole strength,

then give me what is left, and it is wise,

and kind, and steady as a flame.

You mend my small and tangled days.

You never ask for thanks. So let

me say it here in all the ways

a poem can: I see you, love.

I see the work, the care, the grace.

If Christmas ever needed proof

of angels, I would show your face.

2

One of a Kind

written while the first lights went up

They make the stars by the thousand,

the tinsel by the mile.

But no one made another you.

The mould was lost the while

you learned to laugh so loudly,

to love so quietly each day.

Of all the gifts beneath the tree,

you are the one that came to stay.

Let the shops keep every ribbon.

Let the sleigh go on its way.

I have the only treasure

that the season could display.

3

December, Yours

the long glowing weeks

Now the house is wreathed in light

and every evening smells of pine.

I count the days, and every one

is better if it holds your hand in mine.

You take care of everyone you love

so well they never see the cost.

This month, let someone care for you.

Let me be the arms where nothing is lost.

Rest your head. The snow can wait.

The world can wait. The bells can too.

For once, the one who gives and gives

will be the one who is held. That is you.

4

When the Bells Come

Christmas Eve, almost

The tree is tall. The house is hushed.

The stockings hang like sleeping birds.

I hold your hand beneath the lights

and cannot find the proper words,

so let me use the plainest ones:

thank you for the months we've had,

for every quiet kindness shown,

for every hard day made less sad.

When the bells come, let them find us

just like this, in lamplight, near,

a year of loving folded small

and tucked inside another year.

Merry Christmas, my love

5

Christmas is almost here.

And so are you, as you have been all year.

The End

Turn the page to close the book.

Merry Christmas,
my love

The book is closed, but the love goes on.
